# AlumBridge — Education That Guarantees the Future

AlumBridge is a bold, centralized platform designed to reconnect generations of university graduates and empower current students. It serves as a digital bridge between the academic world and the professional landscape, ensuring that the value of education extends far beyond graduation day.

## 🌟 The Vision

AlumBridge solves the "LinkedIn Void" problem. Instead of graduates disappearing into a sea of millions of strangers, AlumBridge creates an exclusive, high-energy home for your specific university community. It’s a place where fast-tracking your career through alumni connections isn't just possible—it's the standard.

## 🚀 How It Works

The platform follows a simple, high-impact loop:

1.  **Verified Onboarding**: Users join using university-linked authentication. Profiles are categorized as either **Student** or **Alumni**.
2.  **The Connection**: Students browse the directory to find mentors in their dream fields. Alumni search for former classmates or browse for fresh talent to join their companies.
3.  **Active Engagement**: 
    *   **Jobs**: Alumni post "warm" opportunities.
    *   **Mentorship**: Direct request system for career guidance.
    *   **Events**: Real-world and digital mixers, reunions, and webinars.
4.  **Growth**: The community strengthens itself as students become alumni and return to mentor the next generation.

## 🛠 Core Features

### 1. Global Alumni Directory
Search and filter through the entire network by graduation year, degree program, current company, or geographic location. Reconnecting with your cohort or finding a local alum in a new city is a one-click process.

### 2. Exclusive Job Board
A dedicated marketplace for opportunities. Alumni can post openings at their own companies, giving fellow graduates a "warm lead" and a direct line to recruitment.

### 3. Structured Mentorship
Bridge the gap between senior graduates and ambitious students. Our structured mentorship request system allows students to seek career advice directly from those who have already walked the path.

### 4. University Events & Reunions
Never miss a campus milestone. From local city mixers and professional webinars to grand decadal reunions, stay informed and RSVP in a single, fluid interface.

### 5. AI-Powered Suggestions
Using **Genkit**, AlumBridge analyzes your profile and career goals to suggest the most relevant alumni mentors or job opportunities, making the "bridge" smarter over time.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy: "Coral & Ink"

AlumBridge uses a high-contrast, youthful aesthetic designed to mirror the energy of a thriving campus:
- **Bold Typography**: Using *Bebas Neue* for high-impact headings.
- **Fluid Motion**: Powered by *Framer Motion* for "bouncy" and "flowing" transitions between views.
- **Dark Mode First**: A sophisticated "Ink" background with "Coral" accents for a modern, professional yet approachable feel.

## 💻 The Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React (Vite) for blazing-fast performance.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 4.0 with a custom OKLCH color palette.
- **Components**: Radix-based ShadCN UI for accessibility and precision.
- **Backend & Auth**: Supabase for real-time data, secure authentication, and PostgreSQL.
- **AI Layer**: Genkit for intelligent community matching and content moderation.
- **Animations**: Framer Motion for a playful, "native" application feel.
