# AlumBridge — Education That Guarantees the Future

AlumBridge is a bold, centralized platform designed to reconnect generations of university graduates and empower current students. It serves as a digital bridge between the academic world and the professional landscape, ensuring that the value of your education extends far beyond graduation day.

## 🌟 What is AlumBridge?

AlumBridge solves the "LinkedIn Void" problem. Instead of graduates disappearing into a sea of millions, AlumBridge creates an exclusive, focused home for your university community. It’s a place where fast-tracking your career through alumni connections isn't just possible—it's the standard.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Global Alumni Directory
Search and filter through the entire network by graduation year, degree program, current company, or geographic location. Reconnecting with your cohort or finding a local alum in a new city has never been easier.

### 2. Exclusive Job Board
A dedicated marketplace for opportunities. Alumni can post openings at their own companies, giving fellow graduates and students a "warm lead" and a direct line to recruitment.

### 3. Mentorship & Guidance
Bridge the gap between senior graduates and ambitious students. Our structured mentorship request system allows students to seek career advice directly from those who have already walked the path.

### 4. University Events & Reunions
Never miss a campus milestone. From local city mixers and professional webinars to grand decadal reunions, stay informed and RSVP in a single click.

### 5. Verified Profiles
Tied to university authentication, every profile is real. Show off your career progression, update your bio, and link your professional socials to stay visible.

## 🛠 The Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React with Vite for high-speed development and performance.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with a custom "Coral & Ink" theme for a bold, youthful aesthetic.
- **UI Components**: ShadCN UI (Radix-based) for accessible, pixel-perfect interfaces.
- **Backend**: Supabase for secure Auth, real-time Database, and reliable data management.
- **Animations**: Framer Motion for fluid, "playful" transitions and interactive elements.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (Sans) for readability and Bebas Neue (Display) for high-impact headings.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

- **Bold & Youthful**: We use high-contrast colors and heavy display type to mirror the energy of a thriving campus.
- **Flowing UX**: Smooth transitions and a step-based approach to complex tasks.
- **Mobile First**: Designed to be useful on a laptop in a library or on a phone in a coffee shop.

## 📂 Project Structure

- `src/pages/`: Contains the main routes (Dashboard, Directory, Jobs, etc.).
- `src/components/ui/`: Reusable, atomic UI components.
- `src/lib/`: Core logic for Authentication and Supabase integration.
- `src/styles.css`: Global theme definitions and utility classes.
